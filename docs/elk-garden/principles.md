# Product Principles

ELK Garden is built as a **scalable commercial product** — not a personal experiment. These principles guide **what** we prioritize; the **style guide** defines **how** that shows up in the UI.

## Experience

- **Simple enough for beginners, capable enough to grow with the user** — no dumbing down; no unnecessary complexity.
- **Clarity first** — users always know where they are, what matters now, and what to do next.
- **Consistency builds trust** — familiar patterns beat clever one-offs.
- **Calm but capable** — supportive tone without sounding fragile; serious enough for real planning and execution.
- **Explain the “why”** — short educational context where it increases confidence and follow-through.
- **Reduce overwhelm** — show summaries and progressive disclosure before dense controls.

## Context

- **Mobile-first, real-world usage** — garden, dirt, sun, intermittent attention.
- **Every meaningful feature ties to outcomes** — more food, less waste, less guesswork, better timing.

## Product thinking

- **Design for longevity** — choices should survive more users, surfaces, and business models without embarrassment.
- **Trust is part of the roadmap** — unclear or sloppy UI undermines adoption and monetization later.
